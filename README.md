# Building AI Agents — course material

Everything runs in your browser. Nothing to install, and no administrator rights.

**Before you start.** Open any notebook below, then add the course key once :
click the key icon in the left sidebar, name the secret `COURSE_API_KEY`, paste
the key the trainer gives you, and switch *Notebook access* on. The key lives in
your Google account, not in the notebook.

Full instructions, and what to do when something fails :
[notebooks/SETUP-COLAB.md](notebooks/SETUP-COLAB.md)

Do not paste anything confidential from your employer into these notebooks. The
exercises never need real data.
